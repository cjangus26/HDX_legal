HDX Inspect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Who we are

HDX Inspect (“the app”) is published by Cameron Angus, an individual developer (sole trader). In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Cameron Angus.

Contact for any privacy question: cameronjohnangus@hotmail.co.uk.

HDX Inspect is a machinery inspection, sign-off, scheduling and reporting tool for professional use by inspection organisations and their staff.

The short version

  • We operate no servers and collect no data. The app has no backend run by us. We receive no analytics, no crash reports, no telemetry, no personal data, and no usage data from the app.
  • Your data stays under your organisation's control. Everything the app records is stored (1) on the device, in a local database encrypted at rest, and (2) if your organisation enables sync, in your organisation's own Google Drive storage — an account we have no access to.
  • No tracking, no advertising, no third-party analytics. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Data the app stores on your behalf

The app records the data your organisation enters while performing machinery inspections:

  • User accounts — name, username, role (administrator or inspector), and a password hash (passwords are hashed with Argon2id; the app never stores your password in plain text).
  • Inspection records — customers, machines, checklists, inspection results, defects, notes, bookings and schedules.
  • Photos — pictures you attach to inspection records, taken with the camera or chosen from your photo library. The app only accesses the camera or photo library when you choose to add a photo.
  • Signatures — sign-off signatures drawn on the device by the inspector and the customer representative.
  • Certificates and reports — PDF documents the app generates from inspection records.
  • Activity log — a record of significant actions in the app (for example who finalised or voided a certificate), kept for accountability within your organisation.

This data is processed for one purpose: performing and documenting machinery inspections for your organisation. It is not used for profiling, marketing, or any secondary purpose, and it is never sold or shared by us — we never possess it.

Where the data lives

  1. On the device. Data is stored in a local database encrypted at rest (SQLCipher/AES). The encryption key is held in the operating system's secure storage (iOS Keychain). Deleting the app deletes this local data; the app also provides an administrator-gated “wipe this device” function.
  2. In your organisation's Google Drive (optional, “connected mode”). If your organisation connects a Google account, the app synchronises the dataset, photos, signatures and certificate PDFs to a folder structure in that Google Drive. The app requests only the narrow drive.file permission (access to files and folders the app itself created — not your whole Drive). Google's processing of data stored in Google Drive is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy. Who can reach that Drive folder is controlled by your organisation's Google account and sharing settings — this is the real data-access boundary.

We — the app publisher — cannot see, access, or recover any of this data.

Data shared with third parties

  • Google (only in connected mode): signing in with Google and storing your organisation's data in its own Google Drive, as described above. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • No one else. There are no other third-party services, SDKs, analytics providers, or advertisers.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — only to take photos you attach to inspection records.
  • Photo library — only to pick existing photos you attach to inspection records.

Both are optional; the app functions without them if you never attach photos.

Retention and deletion

  • Data retention is controlled by your organisation's administrator. The app includes an administrator-configurable retention period after which old records are purged, and administrators can deactivate or delete user accounts. A signed-in user can also delete their own account from within the app (Settings → Delete my account).
  • To have your personal data corrected or deleted, contact your organisation's administrator — under data-protection law (e.g. the GDPR), your organisation is the data controller for inspection data; we are not a processor of it because we never receive it.
  • Deleting the app removes the local database. Data your organisation synced to its own Google Drive is under the organisation's control and can be deleted there.

Children

HDX Inspect is a professional business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the “last updated” date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact
